# Solution Concept

---

## 1. Concept Name

**The EV Tipping Point Finder**
*"See where your city sits in the transition — and what switching to electric actually saves you."*

---

## 2. One-Paragraph Pitch

Marlowe's data shows that across Central and Southern Europe, large populations are still buying petrol and diesel cars in markets where BEV share sits at 3–8% — far below the EU mean of 14.5% — despite registering hundreds of thousands of new passenger cars annually. These aren't people who've rejected EVs; they're people who haven't yet been shown a credible, affordable path in. The EV Tipping Point Finder is an interactive web tool that takes a user's city and current car situation, locates their country's BEV share position on the EU adoption curve (anchored to Marlowe's 2022→2023 data), and then calculates their personal financial case for switching to a CityVolt — showing exactly how far behind the curve they are, and exactly what it costs them to stay there each month.

---

## 3. Who It's For and the Decision It Helps Them Make

**Persona:** A 28–45 year-old urban commuter in Warsaw, Bucharest, Budapest, Madrid, or Lisbon. They own or are about to buy a second-hand petrol city car (sub-€15,000 budget). They're aware EVs exist but assume they're expensive, unproven in their country, or not yet "ready" for someone like them. They are price-sensitive, not early-adopter-motivated.

**Decision:** *"Is right now — in my specific city, with my specific commute and budget — actually a smart time for me to switch to an electric car?"* The tool helps them move from vague awareness that EVs are growing to a concrete, personalised answer: yes, here's the month your savings break even; here's where your country sits on the curve; here's what CityVolt costs you per day.

---

## 4. Core Features to Build

**Screen 1 — Country Position Card**
- User selects their country from a dropdown (pre-populated with the underpenetrated markets Marlowe flagged: CEE + Southern EU)
- Tool displays that country's estimated BEV share (e.g., "Your country: ~5% EV share") plotted as a marker on a simple EU adoption spectrum bar — anchored at the left by the 3–8% cluster, marked at 14.5% (EU mean, 2023), and at the right by the high-penetration outliers (~80%)
- A single callout: *"85.5% of new cars sold across the EU last year were not electric. In your country, it's closer to 95%."* — This is the "you are early, not late" framing, directly from Marlowe's finding
- No login, no form yet — immediate value on load

**Screen 2 — Commute & Cost Input (3 fields max)**
- Monthly km driven in the city
- Current monthly fuel spend (€, estimated or entered)
- How they currently own a car: buying new / buying used / considering first car

**Screen 3 — Personal Savings Output**
- Monthly cost of CityVolt (estimated lease/purchase installment, fixed placeholder for prototype)
- Monthly fuel cost they'd eliminate
- Net monthly saving / cost difference, expressed as €/month and €/day
- Break-even month displayed on a 36-month timeline bar
- One contextual line tied to the compact goods vehicle finding: *"City delivery fleets in your region already run on electric — the charging infrastructure your commute needs already exists."* (This is the de-risking signal from Marlowe's 21.3% light goods BEV share finding, translated for a consumer)

**Screen 4 — Call to Action**
- Single button: *"Reserve a CityVolt in [City]"* — captures city + email
- Below it: a live counter showing "X people in [Country] made the switch last year" using the absolute registration numbers from the dataset (e.g., Poland/Romania/Spain aggregates from the 300,000–400,000 new car figure Marlowe cited)

**Data / Config requirements for the builder:**
- A small static JSON of ~8–10 countries with: estimated BEV share %, 2023 new car registrations (total), approximate BEV registrations — derived from Marlowe's dataset bands
- CityVolt vehicle cost: single configurable placeholder (monthly estimate)
- Average EU electricity cost per 100km vs. petrol cost per 100km: static inputs, editable
- No backend required for prototype; all calculation runs client-side

---

## 5. How It Ties Back to Marlowe's Finding

Every screen is load-bearing on the specific data Marlowe surfaced:

| Tool Element | Marlowe's Finding It Uses |
|---|---|
| The adoption spectrum bar (Screen 1) | EU BEV share = 14.5% in 2023; underpenetrated cluster at 3–8%; range floor to ceiling (~0.5%→81%) |
| "85.5% of new cars are still not electric" callout | Direct inversion of the 14.5% share figure — the non-saturated market argument |
| Country selection limited to CEE + Southern EU | Marlowe's identification of 300–400K annual registrations at 4–8% BEV share as the primary addressable segment |
| The fleet infrastructure reassurance line (Screen 3) | Marlowe's compact light goods BEV share at 21.3% > passenger cars at 14.5% — urban charging infrastructure is already de-risked by fleet operators |
| The "X people switched last year" counter (Screen 4) | The +37% YoY absolute growth figure (418,000 additional BEVs in one year) translated into social proof at country level |

The tool would be incoherent without this specific finding. A generic landing page could exist for any EV brand in any market. This tool only makes sense for a low-cost city EV targeting the exact structural gap Marlowe identified: high car-buying activity, low EV penetration, urban use case already validated by adjacent commercial vehicle data.
